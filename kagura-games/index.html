01   What we saw at Kagura Games02 / 09

Nobody buys a game on a publisher store by accident. They come for a better price or an early release, and they need the store to feel as simple and as safe as Steam.

So every page has one job: look like one shop, in one currency, that is worth buying from directly.

The gap

On one visit from Germany, kaguragames.com showed prices in four currencies: RM in the homepage cart, Rp on The Forbidden Tomes of Olipos, $ on Love Left Behind, and € in every product grid around them.

02   Findings03 / 09

Where kaguragames.com could convert harder.

01

Four currencies in one visit

From one German connection the cart read RM0.00 on the homepage, Rp0.00 on Olipos and $0.00 on Love Left Behind, while every product grid stayed in €. A shopper who sees Rp46,399.20 for an RPG does not convert it in their head, they leave.

02

Your own banners sell Steam

The Olipos page opens on a video banner reading Wishlist Now with the Steam logo, and all four homepage news tiles carry the same Steam badge. On your own store, the biggest artwork points the buyer somewhere else. We would test a buy here version with your price.

03

No price before the third screen

On a phone the homepage opens with a banner and four news posts. The first price sits 1,930 px down, on the third screen, and the UP TO 80% OFF sale only comes after the news. We would test leading with the sale and the bestsellers.

04

The coupon lives on one page

OCTOBER2026, 5% off at checkout, shows only in a bar on the Shop page. The homepage, the Kagura Games category and the product pages carry the code in their HTML but never display it. A shopper who lands on a game from search never sees it.

04   First tests05 / 09

Three tests. Ready to run.

01

One currency

Pick the currency once per visitor, then show it in the cart, the price and every grid on every page.

Measured by: add to cart rate, checkout rate

02

Buy here banners

The same key art on product pages, with your price and a Buy now button instead of Wishlist on Steam.

Measured by: revenue per product page visit

03

Sale first homepage

Open with UP TO 80% OFF, the bestsellers and the OCTOBER2026 code, then the news.

Measured by: clicks to product pages, revenue per visitor

The math

At 138,000 visits a month, every 0.1 point of conversion rate is about 138 more orders a month, from traffic you already have.

Every idea runs as an A/B test first. Only the winners ship.
