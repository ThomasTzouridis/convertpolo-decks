Findings03
02 · Findings

Four leaks.
Zero guesswork.

Leak 01

Proof sits one click deep

The homepage opens on a question, "Balance gewinnen oder Gewicht verlieren?", with two buttons of equal weight. 11,000,000 meals sold and 10,000 Starter-Sets in two months only appear after a click.

Leak 02

Three choices before the cart

The Starter-Set asks for three separate flavour picks before the cart. On a phone, the first screen shows no price and no button. A preselected best seller mix is a fast test.

Leak 03

Week four has no next step

A four week program, yet the page shows no subscribe or reorder option, although SHEKO runs subscriptions (Abo verwalten in the footer). Week four is when the customer decides.

Leak 04

The discount waits at the bottom

The secret discount and the 60 page metabolism guide sit at the very end of the homepage, after the reviews. Higher up and on product pages, they could turn more visitors into a list.
