02 · Findings

Four things we would change first

01

Proof sits one click deep

The homepage opens on "Balance gewinnen oder Gewicht verlieren?" with two equal buttons. The 11,000,000 meals sold and 10,000 Starter-Sets in two months only appear after a click. We would test leading with them.

02

Three choices before the cart

The Starter-Set asks for three separate flavour picks before it can go in the cart, and on a phone the first screen shows no price and no button. A preselected best seller mix is a fast test.

03

Week four has no next step

The Starter-Set is a four week program, yet its page shows no subscribe or reorder option, although SHEKO runs subscriptions. Week four is when the customer decides, so the next step belongs on the page.

04

The discount waits at the bottom

The secret discount and the 60 page metabolism guide sit at the very end of the homepage, after the reviews. Testing the offer higher up and on product pages could turn more visitors into a list.
