02 . Findings

Four leaks.Zero guesses.

Every point below comes from sheko.com itself. Not a template. Not a checklist. Your store.

01

Proof sits one click deep

The homepage opens on "Balance gewinnen oder Gewicht verlieren?" with two equal buttons. The 11,000,000 meals and the 10,000 Starter-Sets in two months only appear after a click. We would test leading with them.

02

Three choices before the cart

The Starter-Set asks for three separate flavour picks before the cart, and on a phone the first screen shows no price and no button. A preselected best seller mix with a simple change option is a fast test.

03

Week four has no next step

A four week program, yet the page shows no subscribe or reorder option, although SHEKO runs subscriptions (Abo verwalten in the footer). Week four is when the customer decides.

04

The discount waits at the bottom

The secret discount and the 60 page metabolism guide sit at the very end of the homepage, after the reviews. Testing the same offer higher up could turn more visitors into a list.

Three tests.No opinions.

A

Proof first homepage

Open with 11,000,000 meals sold and the 4.8 rating, one main button to the Starter-Set.

Measured byClicks to product pages, revenue per visitor

B

One tap Starter-Set

Preselect the three most popular flavours, put price and button in the first mobile screen.

Measured byMobile add to cart rate

The week four bridge
