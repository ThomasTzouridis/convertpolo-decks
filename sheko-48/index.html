FindingsFour opportunities04 / 10

Four moments where orders slip away, and how we would win them back.

01

Proof sits one click deep

The homepage opens on "Balance gewinnen oder Gewicht verlieren?" with two equal buttons. 11,000,000 meals and 10,000 Starter-Sets in two months only appear after a click. We would test leading with them.

02

Three choices before the cart

The Starter-Set asks for three flavour picks before the cart, and on a phone the first screen shows no price and no button. A preselected best seller mix with a simple change option is a fast test.

03

Week four has no next step

A four week program, yet the page shows no subscribe or reorder option, although SHEKO runs subscriptions. Week four is when the customer decides, so the next step belongs on the page.

04

The discount waits at the bottom

The secret discount and the 60 page metabolism guide sit at the very end of the homepage. Testing the same offer higher up and on product pages could turn more visitors into a list.
