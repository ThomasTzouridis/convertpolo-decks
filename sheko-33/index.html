02 · Findings

Great product.Hidden proof.

01

Proof sits one click deep

The homepage opens on a question, "Balance gewinnen oder Gewicht verlieren?", with two buttons of equal weight. The 11,000,000 meals sold and the 10,000 Starter-Sets in two months only appear after a click. We would test leading with them.

02

Three choices before the cart

The Starter-Set asks for three separate flavour picks before it can go in the cart, and on a phone the first screen shows no price and no button. A preselected best seller mix with a simple change option is a fast test.

03

Week four has no next step

The Starter-Set is a four week program, yet its page shows no subscribe or reorder option, although SHEKO runs subscriptions (Abo verwalten in the footer). Week four is when the customer decides.

04

The discount waits at the bottom

The secret discount and the 60 page metabolism guide sit at the very end of the homepage, after the reviews. Testing the same offer higher up and on product pages could turn more visitors into a list.

03 · Buzzword alarm

The CRO glossary.Abridged. Honestly.

Our industry has a word for everything except proof. Here is what the buzzwords actually mean, and what we do instead.

Best practice

noun

Somebody else's winning test, on somebody else's visitors. Yours never voted.

Growth hack

noun

A trick that worked once, for someone else, in a screenshot. We prefer an A/B test.

Gut feeling

noun

Fast, free and very confident. We use statistical significance. Less fun at parties.

Quick win

noun

A guess with a deadline. Ours run as tests first. Only the winners ship.

Data driven

adjective

Something every agency says. We read analytics, session recordings and the voice of the customer. Then we test.

Redesign

noun

Changing everything at once, then guessing what worked. We change one thing and measure it. What a concept.

What we say instead. Hypothesis. Test. Winner.
