Findings

Four openings.Zero excuses.

01

Proof sits one click deep

The homepage opens on a question, "Balance gewinnen oder Gewicht verlieren?", with two buttons of equal weight. The 11,000,000 meals and 10,000 Starter-Sets in two months only appear after a click.

02

Three choices before the cart

The Starter-Set asks for three separate flavour picks before it can go in the cart, and on a phone the first screen shows no price and no button.

Starter-Set, mobile
03

Week four has no next step

A four week program, yet its page shows no subscribe or reorder option, although SHEKO runs subscriptions. Week four is when the customer decides.

04

The discount waits at the bottom

The secret discount and the 60 page metabolism guide sit at the very end of the homepage, after the reviews. Higher up, they could turn more visitors into a list.
